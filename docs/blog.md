## Background
My grandparents' house in the village of Shankaranarayana was demolished a decade ago. The neighborhood was beautiful with a large lake at the center, and a temple next to it. They contained precious memories from my childhood. In 2011, I was aware that the house might be torn down as it was too old, so I took plenty of photos in and around the house, including the temple next door. 

Now with the advent of powerful coding agents like GPT-6 and Opus 5.5 and people showing off their 3D modelling capabilities, I thought let me see if they can re-create my childhood places from photos. 

## Process
I started out by drawing a rough floormap of the house using Procreate on my iPad. I downloaded all the important photos, about 100 of them in a separate directory. I started a session and set a goal to create the 3D version and keep improving the fidelity. It ran overnight and created a pretty decent first version. After that I had to inform it of several corrections and it kept on fixing those. I used the ChatGPT Sites to deploy it as a webapp so that my relatives can experience it easily on their phones. 

## Alignment
Initially I renamed many of the photos by hand, describing the rough location and angle as file names. Later I created an align tool that lets me place the photo in the 3D world itself, making the process much easier. 

## Game creation
Later I created a more photo realistic desktop version by using Blender and Unreal. These re-use the same 3D model, but add detailed meshes. I added gamepad support. Now I can play this like a game. I feel like the characters in Permutation City who live in a virtual world from their memory. 

## Release
I have shared the source code on Github and the photos without people(CC BY 4). Feel free to re-create your own child places and re-live in them.
